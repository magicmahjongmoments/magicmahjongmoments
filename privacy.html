Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Magic Mahjong Moments (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with the Magic Mahjong Moments online storefront (the “Site”). By using the Site or placing an order you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Scope

This policy covers information collected through the Site only. It does not apply to information collected directly by third parties (for example, payment processors or shipping carriers) except as described below.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide at checkout

When you place an order we collect the billing and shipping information required to fulfill that order, such as name, delivery address, billing address, and phone number if provided at checkout. We collect only the information necessary to process and ship your order.

Payment information

We do not collect or store full payment card details. All payment transactions are processed by Stripe. Any payment card details or other payment-specific information are provided directly to and stored by Stripe in accordance with their systems and policies. We may receive confirmation of payment and basic payment metadata (e.g., transaction ID, last 4 digits, payment status) from Stripe to manage your order.

Automatically collected information

When you visit the Site we may automatically collect certain technical information about your device and use of the Site, including IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, referring URLs, and other diagnostic and usage data. This information is collected through server logs and cookies and is used to operate and improve the Site.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and third-party services we use may place cookies, pixels, and similar technologies on your device. These help with site functionality, fraud prevention, performance measurement, and analytics. You may control cookie settings through your browser, but disabling certain cookies may affect Site functionality.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill orders (billing, shipping, order confirmations and order history).
  • To process refunds, exchanges, and cancellations.
  • To investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, or other prohibited uses.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and our services (including troubleshooting, data analysis and testing).
  • To comply with legal obligations (for example, tax or recordkeeping requirements).

We do not use personal data for marketing communications from Magic Mahjong Moments unless you explicitly opt into a separate communication channel that you set up via the Site (the Site does not collect or retain a customer mailing list by default).

4. With whom we share information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers and vendors. Third parties that perform services on our behalf (payment processors such as Stripe, shipping carriers, fulfillment centers, fraud-prevention providers, hosting and analytics services). These parties are contractually limited to using data only as necessary to provide their services.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law or in response to lawful requests by public authorities, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Magic Mahjong Moments, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, financing, or similar transaction involving Magic Mahjong Moments, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Third-party services and links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites and may use third-party services (for example Stripe, shipping carriers, analytics providers). Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for their practices. Payment processing and related records are governed by the payment processor’s policies.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Site and our services, to comply with our legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements. Order and transaction records are retained for tax and accounting purposes for a period required by law. Non-identifying, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information appropriate to the nature of the data. However, no internet-based system is completely secure. If you believe your order or payment information has been compromised, contact us via the Site contact form and provide relevant order details so we can investigate.

8. Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected data from a child in violation of this policy, contact us so we can delete the information.

9. International visitors

The Site is operated from the United States (North Carolina). If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, processed, and stored in the United States. By using the Site and providing information, you consent to such transfers. If you are located in a jurisdiction with data-transfer restrictions, please contact us through the Site contact form to discuss.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information (unless retention is required for legal or business purposes).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your personal information in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, submit a request via the Site contact form and include enough information for us to identify your order or records (for example, order number and date). We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy with a new effective date. Continued use of the Site after posting constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. How to contact us

To ask questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your rights, please use the contact form on the Site and include relevant order details or the nature of your request. We will respond using the channel you provide through the contact form.

By using the Site you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
